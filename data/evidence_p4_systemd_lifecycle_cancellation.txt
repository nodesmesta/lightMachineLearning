Implementation Summary
----------------------
File changed:
  sydeco_lightml_core/worker.py

SystemdTransientWorkerHost.stream() now:
  - snapshots stream identity when the stream begins:
      - unit name;
      - launch count;
      - per-generation worker secret;
  - treats changed identity, stopped worker, or relaunched worker as stale;
  - checks staleness before reads, before yielding chunks, after yielding
    chunks, and before accepting a completed terminal frame;
  - closes the old connection and raises StreamRestarted when the active stream
    belongs to an invalidated worker generation;
  - handles GeneratorExit from client/generator cancellation by closing the
    connection, auditing STREAM_CANCELLED, and triggering scoped recycle through
    the existing _recycle_after_timeout() lifecycle path.
