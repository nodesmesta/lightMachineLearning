Implementation Summary
----------------------
File changed:
  sydeco_lightml_core/worker.py

SystemdTransientWorkerHost.stream() now:
  - reads stream timeout limits from the app manifest resource_limits via the
    existing _stream_timeouts() helper;
  - starts a monotonic stream clock with time.monotonic();
  - computes an absolute total-duration deadline before every frame read;
  - applies first-chunk timeout before the first stream frame;
  - applies idle timeout after the first chunk;
  - applies the smaller of idle/first and remaining total timeout as the socket
    read timeout;
  - sets timeout on both the HTTPConnection socket and the HTTPResponse raw
    socket when available;
  - closes the connection, audits INFERENCE_TIMEOUT, recycles the affected
    worker, and raises StreamTimeout(reason, ...) on deadline violation.

stream deadlines independently from the ordinary inference timeout. The total
deadline is monotonic and absolute, so a worker cannot stream indefinitely by
