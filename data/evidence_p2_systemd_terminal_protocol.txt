Scope

Fix the private worker-to-Core /stream terminal protocol for the
SystemdTransientWorkerHost production path. This section is intentionally
limited to frame semantics and fail-closed parsing; streaming deadlines,
restart invalidation, cancellation, and backpressure remain P3/P4/P5 work.


Implementation Summary
----------------------
Files changed:
  sydeco_lightml_core/worker_runtime.py
  sydeco_lightml_core/worker.py
  tests/test_streaming_systemd_auth.py
  tests/test_streaming_systemd_parity.py

Private internal frame model implemented:
  {"type": "chunk", "data": <adapter chunk>}
  {"type": "completed"}
  {"type": "error", "error": {"code": "500", "message": "internal error"}}

Rules implemented:
  - Adapter chunks are carried only by type=chunk frames.
  - Normal worker stream completion is explicit: type=completed.
  - Adapter/runtime stream errors are explicit: type=error.
  - EOF before completed/error fails closed.
  - Malformed internal frames fail closed.
  - Public/internal details stay sanitized; internal adapter exception text is
    not forwarded by the private stream frame parser.
